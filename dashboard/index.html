How the radar scores

  1. Eligible: the record carries no token (no CoinGecko id, no symbol), and it is not a wrapped, staked or bridged product of a token that already trades, nor an exchange, nor a versioned pool. The house watchlist’s own rule, applied to the whole snapshot instead of only above its $50M floor. Every watchlist slug is on the board; the rest are the early rows.
  2. Category ratio: the median market-cap-per-TVL of the live-token protocols in the same category (five peers or more, else the snapshot-wide median). What the market already pays per dollar of TVL for this kind of protocol.
  3. Implied value = TVL × category ratio × growth × age. Growth is the seven-day TVL change, clamped to −50…+150% and weighted 0.6. Age is how long the protocol has run with no token, 0.8…1.2 over four years. A protocol the record says nothing about is scored at one year of age and zero growth.
  4. Farm plan: six steps per protocol, derived from its own category, chains, TVL and age — what to do, what it costs, how long it takes. Nothing here predicts whether or when a token is issued; a protocol on the board may still have a token its record does not carry.
